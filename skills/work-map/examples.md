## See what a piece of work touches, before or after it

Changes: nothing; it goes into an issue or a pull request only on your yes.

| You type | When | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/v2:work-map planned 1320` | before starting an epic or an issue | a diagram of what it will touch, then the same in words |
| `/v2:work-map done` | the work of this branch is done | the diagram compared with its plan: what was not done, what was not planned |
| `/v2:work-map glossary` | the hierarchy of the glossary | contains, is a, refers to; dashed without a source, red where the data says otherwise |
| "fammi vedere cosa cambia" | in the middle of a conversation | the map of the work of this conversation |
