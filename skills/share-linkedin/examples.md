## Tell people about a product once it ships

Changes: the files of the video; a post only on your words naming its id.

| You type | When | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/v2:share-linkedin https://example.com/studio` | a product is live | a 1080x1350 video as the cover of an article, its title, its body and the text that shares it; you publish it |
| `/v2:share-linkedin https://example.com/studio the tokens playground` | the product does not say what it is | the same, from your description |
| `/v2:share-linkedin <url>` then "un post con il video nativo, domani mattina" | you want a post instead | the post checked and given an id; "pubblica <id>" publishes or schedules it |
