## Know where the work stands and what comes next

Changes: nothing; it only reads.

| You type | When | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/v2:work-status 1320` | an epic is open | its parts in the order they land, the open pull requests with their checks, the next action |
| `/v2:work-status` | in a new conversation | the repository: open epics, issues by subject, pull requests, local worktrees |
| `/v2:work-status 72` | one pull request, issue or branch | what it does in plain words, its state, its map |
| "a che punto siamo?" | in the middle of the work | a question first: the epic of this work, or the whole repository |
